# rune-engine

Rune Engine is an intuitive emotional guidance system powered by the ancient Elder Futhark runes and modern AI logic. Describe your emotional state, and the engine matches you with a rune, a ritual, and a meditation to help realign your energy.

## Features

- Input emotion-based keywords like `frustration`, `joy`, `stuck`, or `inspired`
- Instantly match to one of the 24 Elder Futhark runes
- Get personalized rituals and meditations based on your current state
- See your top 3 rune matches for deeper insight

## How It Works

Rune Engine compares your emotional input to rune profiles using a scoring system. Each rune is linked to specific emotional frequencies (e.g. clarity, transformation, trust, stillness).

You can easily expand the engine with more emotional terms or plug in AI sentiment analysis.
